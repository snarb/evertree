# Engineering Principles

- Prefer the simplest design that fully satisfies current requirements. Minimize nesting, concepts, states, and moving parts.
- Do not add speculative abstractions, configurability, compatibility, migrations, or future-proofing.
- Keep interfaces and data models minimal and elegant: every field, parameter, and option must have a current purpose.
