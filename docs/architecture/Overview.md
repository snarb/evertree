## Introduction

**EverTree** is an architecture designed to create an efficient Artificial General Intelligence (AGI). 
The core components of an EverTree agent's “subconscious” are pretrained large language models (LLMs), which are then further fine-tuned. 

EverTree uses LLMs where they make sense — among other things, to accelerate learning, improve generalization, and transform natural language into concepts and vice versa.
