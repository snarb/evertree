## Introduction

**EverTree** is an architecture designed to create an efficient Artificial General Intelligence (AGI). In many respects, it resembles the structure of the human psyche, adapted for practical use and existing hardware. The system is not limited to a human model; its main criterion is the efficiency of its evolutionary adaptation.

The core components of an EverTree agent's “subconscious” are pretrained large language models (LLMs), which are then further fine-tuned. In their original form, LLMs **contain** general knowledge about the world, language understanding and generation capabilities, and information for solving various tasks. However, their inherent architecture significantly limits them.

EverTree uses LLMs where they make sense — among other things, to accelerate learning, improve generalization, and transform natural language into concepts and vice versa.

---

EverTree contains the following systems:

- **Memory**
- **Cognition/Reflection**
- **Analytical System**
- **Planning**
- **Dynamic World Model**
- **Emotional Regulation**
- **Regulation of System Modes and Cycles** _(switching between long-term states such as exploration, creativity, reflection, experience integration, and maintaining their rhythm)_
- **Mechanism for Mutual Cooperation and Complementarity** _(introduced in version 2; omitted for now)_
- **Natural Selection Mechanism** _(introduced in version 2; omitted for now)_
