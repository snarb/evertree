

## Introduction


**EverTree** is an architecture designed to create an efficient Artificial General Intelligence (AGI). In many aspects, it resembles the structure of the human psyche but is adapted for practical purposes and existing hardware. The system is not limited to a human model; its main criterion is the efficiency of its evolutionary adaptation.

The core components of the “subconscious” in the EverTree agent are pretrained large language models (LLMs), which are then further fine-tuned. LLMs,  their original form **contain** general knowledge about the world, language understanding and generation capabilities,  information for solving various tasks. However, due to their inherent architecture, they are significantly limited.

EverTree uses LLMs where it makes sense — to improve learning speed, enhance generalization, and transform natural language into concepts (and vice versa), among other roles.

---

EverTree contains following systems:

- **Memory**
- **Cognition/Reflection**
- **Analytical System**
- **Planning**
- **Dynamic World Model**
- **Emotional Regulation**
- **Regulation of System Modes and Cycles** _(switching between long-term states, such as exploration, creativity, reflection, experience integration, and maintaining their rhythmicity)_
- **Mechanism for Mutual Cooperation and Complementarity** _(introduced in version 2, omitted for now)_
- **Natural Selection Mechanism** _(introduced in version 2, omitted for now)_
